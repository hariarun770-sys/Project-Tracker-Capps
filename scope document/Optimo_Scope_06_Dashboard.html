1. Functional Scope & Self-Service Engine
  • Unified Reservation Timeline: Chronological overview of citizen's current and past bookings across sports facilities, civic halls, and parking.
  • Dynamic QR Turnstile Pass: Cryptographically generated pass refreshable every 60s for contactless entry at venue gates.
  • Tax Folio & Invoice Portal: Instant download of GST/VAT tax invoices and payment receipts with zero council admin intervention.
  • Automated Cancellation & Reschedule: Self-service rescheduling engine enforcing tiered refund policies based on lead time.
