1. Core User Journeys 4 Defined Paths
Journey 1: Staff Member Issue Reporting (via Concierge)
Staff Reporter
  • Trigger: Staff member encounters a bug or question on any portal page (e.g., Marina Bay venue booking).
  • Entry Point: Opens Concierge (⌘I / Avatar Menu) → clicks "Report an issue" or "Escalate to Support" under an AI reply.
  • Auto-Attached Telemetry: System auto-snapshots active hub, path, active record, console logs, failed network calls, and chat history.
  • Data Entry: Reporter enters Title (≤140 chars) & What happened? (≤4000 chars), attaches up to 3 screenshots and 5 files (≤5MB each).
  • Submission: Validated submission generates sequential reference TKT-000x, sets status New, starts SLA clock, and shows toast.
Journey 2: Support Staff Triage & Assignment Queue
Support Staff
  • Entry Point: Support opens /admin/support/tickets from Governance Hub sidebar or header user menu.
  • Overview Inspection: Triage sees 4 summary KPI tiles: Total (neutral), Open (amber), Blockers (red), Resolved (green).
  • Search & Filter: Free-text filters across title, ref (e.g. TKT-0001), and description; dropdowns for Status & Severity.
  • Ticket Claim: Support opens ticket detail (/admin/support/tickets/:id), inspects telemetry, sets status Triaged, assigns owner.
Journey 3: Collaborative Investigation & Internal Notes
Triage & Assignee
  • Threaded Updates: Staff posts public comments to reporter, or toggles "Internal Note" (hidden from reporter, flagged visually).
  • First Response Clock: Automatically stops when first non-internal comment by non-reporter is posted.
  • Status Flow: Transitions through In progress → Waiting on reporter (if clarification needed) → resumes on reply.
Journey 4: Resolution & Knowledge Base Promotion
Resolved & KB
  • Resolution: Status set to Resolved; system stamps immutable resolvedAt date.
  • Knowledge Capture: Support clicks "Promote to KB" → copies title, description, and non-internal comments to KB.
  • Lifecycle Exit: Ticket permanently links to created article; button hides to prevent duplicate promotion.

Auto-Attached Telemetry Context (Zero Manual Reporter Overhead)
Captured automatically upon clicking "Report an issue" inside Concierge:
Active Hub & URL: /admin/... route & params
Active Record: Venue/Booking ID being viewed
Console Errors: Recent window.console errors
Network Logs: Failed HTTP status (no payloads)
Breadcrumbs: Last 10 interaction steps
Concierge Thread: Prior chat prompt & reply
Workflow State Machine
New → Triaged → In progress → Waiting on reporter → Resolved | Won't fix
• Open: Any status other than Resolved or Won't fix.
• First Response: Auto-logged on 1st public reply by non-reporter.
