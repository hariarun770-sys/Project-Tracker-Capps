1. Citizen & Booking Journeys 4 Core Paths
Journey 1: Venue Discovery & Space Selection
Public Citizen
  • Discovery: Citizen navigates CivicSpaces home UI → searches venues or clicks featured Marina Bay Autodrome banner.
  • Space Catalog: Browses spaces: Paddock Club Lounge (cap 200), Pit Lane Garages (cap 24), Members Lounge (cap 60).
  • Real-Time Schedule: Inspects live interactive timetable for available session slots and track configurations.
  • Basket Assembly: Selects required time slots and adds base space reservation to active booking basket.
Journey 2: Vehicle Fleet Hire & Pit Garage Allocation
Track Hire
  • Equipment Selection: Citizen/Club chooses track vehicle: Porsche 911 GT3 (502 hp), Aston Martin Vantage (510 hp), BMW M2 Competition (460 hp).
  • Pit Lane Add-On: Designates Pit Lane Garage unit (1 to 24) with secure direct circuit access and tool storage.
  • Logistics Coordination: Selects North or South Car Park pass (20 allocated bays each) with vehicle registration plate validation.
Journey 3: Hospitality Catering & VIP Dining Packages
Hospitality
  • Package Selection: Chooses Platinum Restaurant Package ($56.50/head, 40 seats) or Members Lounge ($35/head, 60 seats).
  • Inclusions Check: Inclusions verify climate-controlled panoramic straight view, TAB screen access, and private VIP bar.
Journey 4: Instant Folio, Confirmation & AI Self-Service
Customer Account
  • Checkout: Review line-itemized folio; automated deposit computation and instant digital confirmation receipt.
  • Self-Service Management: Booking displays in Customer Dashboard; AskOptimo conversational AI assists with amendments.
