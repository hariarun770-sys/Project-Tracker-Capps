1. Core Knowledge Journeys 4 Distinct Paths
Journey 1: 1-Click Ticket-to-Article Promotion
Triage Staff
  • Trigger: Staff reviews a support ticket marked Resolved on /admin/support/tickets/:id.
  • Promotion Action: Clicks prominent "Promote to KB" button in ticket action bar.
  • Auto-Generation Engine: System copies ticket title & description; appends public non-internal comments; strips internal notes.
  • Tagging & State: Article auto-tags with ticket category (e.g., bug, how-to) and sets state to Published.
  • Confirmation & Lock: Shows green toast ("Promoted to Knowledge Base"); links article; hides button permanently.
Journey 2: Direct Article Authoring from Scratch
Authoring Staff
  • Entry Point: Staff navigates to /admin/support/kb via KB button on tickets screen or direct URL.
  • Initiation: Clicks "+ New article" top-right button → blank modal dialog opens with Published pre-checked.
  • Content & Preview: Enters Title and body text (formatted in Markdown); real-time rendered preview renders live.
  • Metadata & Save: Adds comma-separated tags (e.g., booking, venue, refund) → clicks "Create article".
  • Feedback: Validates non-blank inputs; saves synchronously; shows "Article created" toast notification.
Journey 3: Lifecycle Management & Curation
Content Curator
  • Search & Filter: Enters keywords into live search box; articles filter dynamically by title, body, and tag chips.
  • Click-to-Edit: Clicks any article row to reopen pre-filled editor dialog; adjusts body or unchecks Published to set Draft.
  • Safe Deletion: Clicks trash icon → browser prompt confirms ("Delete this article?") → permanently removed.
Journey 4: Concierge AI Deflection Loop
AI Assistant
  • Staff Query: User asks Concierge: "How do I reserve the Paddock Club at Marina Bay Autodrome?"
  • Semantic Lookup: Concierge AI scans published KB articles for matches before offering ticket escalation.
  • Deflection Output: AI outputs verified resolution directly → zero ticket filed → triage burden reduced.

AI Deflection & Knowledge Recycling Architecture
Self-Sustaining Deflection Cycle
Ticket Resolved: Support agent solves complex customer or staff issue.
Promote to KB: Resolution converted to published article in 1 click.
AI Assistant Ingestion: Concierge AI reads published articles into query cache.
Deflection Achieved: Future inquiries answered automatically, avoiding repeat tickets.
Core Business Rules
• Mandatory Fields: Title and Body cannot be blank or whitespace-only.
• One-Time Promotion: "Promote to KB" disappears permanently once used on a ticket.
• Strict Privacy: Internal triage comments are never exposed to generated articles.
