1. Functional Scope & Account Entities
  • Citizen & Corporate Directory: Master registry aggregating private residents, community groups, and corporate partners across municipal hubs.
  • Resident Status Verification: Automated address validation against Melbourne council rates database to qualify for municipal subsidies.
  • Folio Aggregation: Real-time tally of historical bookings, active venue hire agreements, and outstanding invoice balances.
  • Direct Impersonation & Support: Admin ability to open a customer's view to assist with booking alterations or troubleshoot issues.
