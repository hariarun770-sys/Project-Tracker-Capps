Release 1.0 • 10 September
Business App Stabilised
A business can run its core in-store operation reliably without depending on fragmented tools or fallback processes.
Key outcomes
  • Orders and payments work reliably end-to-end on the Business App.
  • A guest's orders stay on one bill, regardless of the channel.
  • Staff can complete the core service flow in-app: seating → ordering → service requests → payment.
  • Card payments can be completed directly through the business flow without falling back to a till.
  • Admin can register and activate a business, then configure its own setup.
Staff on mobile

A business can onboard, configure itself, serve guests, manage orders and collect payments through OneNex.
Release 1.1 • 17 September
Full Customer Journey Across App & Web
Customers and staff can complete the full journey across OneNex, with a shared real-time order and payment experience.
Key outcomes
  • The complete customer journey works across Business App and Web: reserve → arrive → dine/order → pay → close.
  • Dine-in, immediate takeaway and click & collect are supported through both customer-initiated and staff-initiated flows.
  • Staff and customers see the same order state in real time.
  • A failed payment can be retried and completed without losing the transaction.
  • The platform is ready for pilot businesses and live demonstrations.
